# Executive AI Opportunity Discovery
**Status:** draft workshop. **Duration:** 90 minutes.
**Objective:** agree on one business problem, the evidence gaps and a next decision.

## Prepare
Invite the business sponsor, process owner, representative users and relevant data, technology and risk stakeholders. Request an approved workflow description and existing baseline evidence. Share the opportunity assessment in advance. Do not place confidential inputs in an unapproved AI tool.

## Agenda
| Minutes | Activity | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10 | Agree on the decision and constraints | Decision question |
| 10–30 | Map workflow friction and its evidence | Problem statement |
| 30–45 | Compare AI and non-AI alternatives | Shortlist |
| 45–65 | Challenge value, feasibility and risks | Assumptions and blockers |
| 65–80 | Define the smallest useful experiment | Draft pilot brief |
| 80–90 | Record decision and owners | Decision log |

## Facilitation questions
- Which decision or task needs to improve, and for whom?
- What do we know from evidence versus opinion?
- What simpler alternative could solve the problem?
- What would make this unsafe, unusable or uneconomic?
- Who will own adoption, evaluation and ongoing operation?
- What result would justify stopping?

**Close:** circulate the problem statement, unresolved disagreements, accountable owners and review date. A workshop agreement is not evidence that the solution works.
