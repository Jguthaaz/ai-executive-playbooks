## Original contribution and scope of reuse
This is an original AI-assisted, vendor-neutral executive synthesis for this repository. The ten-question canvas, numbered selection tree, local A0–A3 authority conventions, scorecard weights/anchors, fictional inputs, sensitivity calculations, architecture brief and validation script were authored for this module. They are not Microsoft criteria, certification or an official adaptation.

Conceptual selection and evaluation guidance was informed by the upstream documents above. No upstream code, diagrams, logos or product comparison tables were copied. The upstream MIT license is reproduced verbatim as an attribution record and to preserve its notice for relevant reuse. It does not establish a new license for unrelated existing repository material.

The existing repository's favorable scoring, mandatory gates, pilot controls and value measurement were reused by reference; see the [integration review](README.md). No separate portfolio-prioritization engine, financial model or operational agent was added.

No Microsoft endorsement, partnership or validation is implied. Product capabilities, lifecycle, regional availability and prices were not verified for implementation; v0.1 makes no product recommendation. Review current official documentation and local requirements before selecting a platform.
