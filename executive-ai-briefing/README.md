# Executive AI Briefing
**Status: Work in Progress · v0.1 · October 2026**

Translate a business question involving AI into a concise, decision-ready executive memo.

[Read the briefing and reusable structure](briefing.md). The fictional committee must decide whether to fund discovery for quotation assistance, with a process-only alternative and clear conditions for further investment.

The project makes executive synthesis, tradeoffs and communication of uncertainty visible. It is not a briefing delivered to a real board or evidence of executive decisions influenced. No current technology, vendor or market claims are made; the memo uses only linked portfolio assumptions.

**Next increment:** ask reviewers whether the decision, alternatives and required evidence are clear, then record actual feedback. Prepared with AI assistance.
