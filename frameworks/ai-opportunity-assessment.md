# AI Opportunity Assessment
**Status:** draft template. **Purpose:** decide whether to investigate, test, defer or reject an opportunity.

## 1. Business problem
- Decision owner: [role]
- Users and workflow: [who does what]
- Problem and supporting evidence: [source and date]
- Current baseline: [measure, or unknown]
- Desired outcome: [observable business change]
- Alternative without AI: [process, search, rules or training]
- Why AI may help: [hypothesis, not conclusion]

## 2. Compare options
| Dimension | Evidence | Uncertainty / next check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business value and strategic fit | [facts] | [unknowns] |
| Data availability and permission | [facts] | [unknowns] |
| Workflow integration and adoption | [facts] | [unknowns] |
| Quality and evaluation feasibility | [facts] | [unknowns] |
| Cost and operational ownership | [facts] | [unknowns] |
| Privacy, security and potential harm | [facts] | [unknowns] |

Use qualitative judgments with reasons; avoid a single score that hides unresolved risks.

## 3. Decision
- Recommendation: [investigate / test / defer / reject]
- Rationale and strongest counterargument: [text]
- Evidence needed to change the decision: [text]
- Blocking conditions: [permissions, data, ownership or evaluation]
- Next action, accountable owner and review date: [text]

**Output:** one defensible decision and a prioritized evidence gap list.
