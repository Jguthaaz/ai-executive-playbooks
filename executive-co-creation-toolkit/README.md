# Executive Co-Creation Toolkit
**Status: Work in Progress · v0.1 · October 2026**

A practical exercise in turning different executive perspectives into a shared problem, a shortlist of opportunities and an accountable next decision.

This is a hypothetical portfolio exercise, not a client engagement or a record of a workshop delivered. All organizations, inputs, scores and sample outputs are invented. No outcomes have been measured.

## Executive brief
A fictional B2B services company wants to improve quotation turnaround without weakening commercial controls. Sales, operations and technology disagree on whether the priority is standardization, workflow redesign or AI assistance. This toolkit structures a 90-minute conversation to expose those differences and define what to investigate.

**Decision at the end:** which opportunity deserves a bounded discovery step, who owns it and what evidence is required? Workshop agreement does not authorize implementation.

## What this exercise makes visible
- Business framing before solution selection.
- Cross-functional alignment with dissent and uncertainty recorded.
- Explicit criteria, human accountability and a small next step.

These are design intentions demonstrated by the artifact, not independently assessed competencies or evidence of business results.

## Scope and evolution
This expands the existing [executive discovery outline](../workshops/executive-discovery.md) with a filled example and a reusable capture sheet. Use the existing [pilot brief](../templates/pilot-brief.md) after evidence supports a test.

- **Now:** draft facilitation pack and constructed example.
- **Next:** rehearse with volunteers using fictional inputs; record timing, confusing questions and disagreements.
- **Later:** revise the canvas and scoring anchors from feedback. Add anonymized evidence only with permission.
