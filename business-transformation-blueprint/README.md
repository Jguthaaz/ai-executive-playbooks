# Business Transformation Blueprint
**Status: Work in Progress · v0.1 · October 2026**

Connect a strategic objective to process redesign, operating responsibilities, capabilities, adoption and staged execution.

## Executive question
How could a B2B services company improve quotation responsiveness while preserving commercial control and establishing evidence before scaling AI?

The blueprint uses a fictional quotation workflow to show how technology fits within a wider business change. Process standardization comes first; AI remains a conditional option.

## Contents
- [Worked transformation blueprint](blueprint.md): current-state hypotheses, target workflow, capability gaps, governance and a 90-day plan.
- [Reusable blueprint template](template.md): a compact structure for another transformation challenge.

This is an independent hypothetical planning exercise, not a transformation delivered, an approved program or evidence of business results. All organization details, timing and targets are invented. Prepared with AI assistance; no confidential data is included.
