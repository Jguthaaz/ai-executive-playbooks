# Growth Opportunity Explorer
**Status: Work in Progress · v0.1 · October 2026**

Explore where a business could grow before selecting a technology or committing to a market.

## Executive question
Which customer problem deserves commercial discovery, and what evidence would justify a small experiment?

[Worked exploration](exercise.md) compares three opportunities for a fictional B2B maintenance provider. It includes segments, jobs to be done, value propositions, commercial assumptions, a comparison matrix and falsifiable discovery tests. The worksheet at the end can be reused.

This hypothetical exercise makes growth strategy and customer-centered innovation reasoning inspectable. It does not establish market demand, a real pipeline or revenue results. All inputs and organizations are invented; no external market research has been conducted.

**Next increment:** challenge the segment definitions and interview plan before conducting any authorized research. No outreach has occurred. Prepared with AI assistance.
