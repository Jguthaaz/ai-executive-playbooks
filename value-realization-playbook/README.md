# Value Realization Playbook
**Status: Work in Progress · v0.1 · October 2026**

Turn an AI value hypothesis into a measurement plan, an accountable benefits register and an explicit scale-or-stop decision.

## Executive question
When does an AI quotation assistant create business value after review effort, adoption and operating costs are considered?

This hypothetical exercise distinguishes released capacity, cash savings and commercial upside. It extends the [quotation assistant concept](../examples/quotation-assistant.md); it is a separate synthetic measurement exercise, not a tested business case or an implementation result.

## Contents
- [Worked value exercise](exercise.md): value tree, baseline, synthetic arithmetic, sensitivity, evaluation and decision gates.
- [Reusable benefits register](template.md): owners, evidence, costs, dependencies and review decisions.

All numbers and thresholds are invented for discussion. No actual pilot, savings, revenue improvement or client delivery is claimed. Prepared with AI assistance; no confidential inputs are used.

## What to look for
A benefit is a hypothesis until supported by evidence. Time released is not automatically cash saved. Adoption and human review belong in the calculation. Scale requires quality and control evidence as well as a plausible economic case.

## Evolution
**Now:** documentation and synthetic scenarios, no code.
**Next:** challenge the baseline, cost assumptions and quality thresholds with reviewers.
**Later:** populate the register with approved evidence and record a real decision only after authorized evaluation.
