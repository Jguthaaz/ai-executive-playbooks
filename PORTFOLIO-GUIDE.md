# Project Overview
Practical exercises connecting business strategy, growth, AI and transformation.

Browse the projects below to see their purpose, contents and relevance. Open a project to read its example and reusable materials.

## Where to start
- **Explore growth:** Growth Opportunity Explorer.
- **Compare AI initiatives:** Decision Intelligence Lab.
- **Plan business change:** Business Transformation Blueprint.
- **Inspect a software prototype:** AI Opportunity Simulator. Its README explains how to run it locally.

Each project can be read independently. The fictional scenarios use different assumptions; they are not completed phases of one engagement.

## About these materials
**Work in Progress.** These are AI-assisted exercises and experimental artifacts. Hypothetical scenarios and synthetic inputs are labeled in each project. They do not document client delivery, implemented transformations or measured business results. No confidential employer or client information is included.
