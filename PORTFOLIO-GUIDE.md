# Portfolio Guide — projects and professional relevance
**Status: Work in Progress · October 2026**

## Purpose and positioning
This portfolio explores the connection between business strategy, growth, innovation, AI and transformation: framing a challenge, aligning perspectives, comparing choices, planning execution and testing whether value is realized.

It complements Jorge Gutierrez Haaz's professional profile by making selected approaches to business problems visible and open to discussion. The descriptions below are positioning themes, not official job titles. Career history, roles and accomplishments should be assessed through the current CV, LinkedIn profile and corroborating evidence.

The four featured projects form a suggested reading sequence:
**Frame opportunities → challenge investment choices → design execution → verify value.**
They share a fictional B2B setting for readability, but they are separate exercises with different assumptions. They are not stages of an actual engagement, nor does a later exercise imply an earlier funding decision passed.

## Suggested routes for readers
- **Strategy and innovation conversations:** co-creation → investment lab → transformation blueprint.
- **Transformation and operating leadership conversations:** blueprint → value realization → workshop.
- **AI strategy and analytics conversations:** investment lab → simulator → value realization.

These routes reflect the content of the artifacts, not a claim that a particular role or qualification has been earned. Recruiters can use them as starting points for questions about judgment, tradeoffs and experience.

## Questions this portfolio invites
- How would the problem definition change when stakeholders disagree?
- Which assumptions would reverse the investment decision?
- What should improve through process redesign before AI is considered?
- Who owns adoption, operating controls and benefits after the pilot?
- When is released capacity valuable, and when does it become a verified cash effect?

## How to interpret maturity and authorship
- **Work in Progress:** usable draft for review; assumptions and structure can change.
- **Hypothetical / synthetic:** constructed scenarios and inputs; no real client, meeting, investment or business outcome.
- **Prototype:** inspectable experimental artifact; not evidence of production operation.
- **Measured result:** use this label only when an actual test, method and supporting evidence are documented.

Materials were prepared with AI assistance. Publication does not establish that every framework has been independently validated or every proposed exercise personally executed. Contributions explicitly recorded in existing cases remain the reference for those cases. No confidential employer or client data is included.
